## ⚠️ ALICE/101 DEFENSE - Critical Elements

### MUST INCLUDE in Specification:
1. **Technical Problem Statement**:
   - "Technical problem of verifying service performance without storing privacy-sensitive raw media"
   - "Technical limitation of existing systems requiring centralized trust"

2. **Technical Solution Description**:
   - "Novel combination of inertial sensor windowing with cryptographic hashing"
   - "Hardware-specific optimizations for mobile device sensors"

3. **Technical Improvement Metrics**:
   - "50% reduction in data storage requirements"
   - "Verification within 500ms latency"
   - "99.5% accuracy in performance classification"

### AVOID in Claims:
- ❌ "Business method" language
- ❌ "On a computer" generic implementations  
- ❌ Abstract descriptions without technical specifics
- ❌ Overly broad claims without hardware details
