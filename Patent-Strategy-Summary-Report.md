## Key Findings and Recommendations

### 1. Strong Patent Potential
Cubiqo's AI browser technology appears to have strong patent potential in three key areas:
- **Privacy-preserving AI processing** (novel approach to local AI)
- **Browser-specific AI integration** (technical framework innovations)
- **Multi-modal content understanding** (real-time analysis techniques)

### 2. Strategic Filing Approach Recommended
**Immediate Action**: File provisional applications within 30 days
**Reason**: Establishes priority dates while technology develops
**Cost**: $9,825-$16,725 for 3 provisional applications

### 3. Cost-Effective Strategy for Startups
- Utilize **Small Entity Status** (50% USPTO fee discount)
- Consider **Micro Entity Status** if qualified (75% discount)
- Start with **provisional applications** to manage costs
- Use **fixed-fee arrangements** with boutique firms

### 4. Risk Mitigation Critical
- **Alice/101 Rejections**: Common for software/AI patents
- **Strategy**: Emphasize technical improvements, specific applications
- **Prior Art**: Conduct comprehensive search before filing
- **Budget**: Establish clear milestones and fixed fees
