## Quick Reference: What Makes a Good Patentable Invention?

### Patent-Eligible Subject Matter (USPTO Guidelines)
- **Technical Solution**: Solves a technical problem
- **Specific Implementation**: Not just an abstract idea
- **Practical Application**: Has real-world utility
- **Novel Combination**: New way of combining existing elements

### Common Pitfalls to Avoid
- **Abstract Ideas**: "Doing X on a computer" is not enough
- **Insufficient Detail**: Must enable someone to reproduce
- **Prior Disclosure**: Public disclosure before filing can void rights
- **Multiple Inventions**: Each distinct invention needs separate application

### AI/ML Specific Considerations
- **Algorithm Details**: Must disclose enough for implementation
- **Training Data**: Describe data sources and preprocessing
- **Model Architecture**: Specific layers, parameters, connections
- **Technical Improvement**: Emphasize technical benefits over business methods
