Investor Outreach Plan

Strategic framework for identifying, contacting, and pitching to investors

🎯 Target Investor Profiles

Types of Investors to Target:

  • Angel Investors: High-net-worth individuals investing personal funds
  • Venture Capital Firms: Early-stage (Seed, Series A) focused funds
  • Corporate Venture Capital: Strategic investors from relevant industries
  • Accelerators/Incubators: Y Combinator, Techstars, etc.
  • Family Offices: Private wealth management for wealthy families
  • Syndicates: AngelList syndicates, Republic, etc.

Ideal Investor Criteria:

  • Investment range: $50K - $2M
  • Industry focus matches your sector
  • Geographic preference aligns with your location
  • Stage preference: Pre-seed to Series A
  • Value-add beyond capital (connections, expertise)

📢 Outreach Channels & Strategy

Primary Channels:

Email (Cold) LinkedIn (Warm) Twitter/X (Engagement) AngelList (Direct) Warm Introductions Events/Conferences Demo Days Investor Updates

Outreach Sequence:

  1. Research: Identify 50-100 target investors with relevant focus
  2. Warm-up: Follow on social media, engage with content (2-3 weeks)
  3. Initial Contact: Personalized email/LinkedIn message
  4. Follow-up: 2-3 follow-ups spaced 5-7 days apart
  5. Meeting Request: Clear call-to-action for 15-30 minute call
  6. Nurture: Add to investor update list regardless of response

🎤 Pitch Framework

Subject Line (Email):
[Your Company Name] - [Brief Value Prop] - Seeking [Amount] for [Purpose]
Opening (30 seconds):
"Hi [Investor Name], I'm [Your Name], founder of [Company Name]. We're building [clear problem statement] for [target market]. We've achieved [key metric/traction] and are raising [amount] to [specific use of funds]."
Problem (1 minute):
"Currently, [target customers] struggle with [specific pain points]. The existing solutions [competitor weaknesses] costing them [quantifiable impact]."
Solution (1 minute):
"Our [product/service] solves this by [unique approach]. Unlike [competitors], we [key differentiators]. Our technology/approach enables [specific benefits]."
Traction (1 minute):
"In [time period], we've achieved: [revenue/users/customers], [growth rate], [partnerships], [awards/recognition]. Our roadmap shows [near-term milestones]."
Ask & Team (30 seconds):
"We're raising [amount] at [valuation] to [specific use of funds]. Our team includes [relevant experience]. We believe your expertise in [investor's focus area] would be valuable."

📋 Action Plan

Success Metrics:

  • Response rate: 10-20%

  • Term sheet rate: 5-10% of meetings
